# Review progress

Judge progress from evidence relevant to the chosen object and horizon. Retrieval, transfer, and application are the shared criteria. Counts and time may explain context, but they do not prove learning or improvement.

## Select the review

Confirm the active vault boundary. Read its agent guide, BP Vault contracts, and the target's related evidence. If the contracts are missing, recommend `setup-bp-vault` before writing a review.

Infer the target and local review mode from the request and method contract. Ask one question when multiple modes would change the evidence or prompts. Load only the relevant branch:

- [Time horizon](./references/time-horizon.md) for Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or another period defined by the vault.
- [Practice or evaluation](./references/practice-or-evaluation.md) for exercise sets, practical work, exams, or readiness checks.

For a note, application, or project review, use the mode mapped by the local contract and stay in this file.

## Review the evidence

1. Gather the evidence sources named by the local mode. Keep searches and links inside the active vault.
2. Separate observed outcomes from the user's interpretation. Surface source or evidence conflicts.
3. Judge retrieval, transfer, and application only where each criterion is relevant. Mark missing evidence as unknown, not failure or success.
4. Identify the strongest evidence, the limiting gap, and a proportional next check or attempt.
5. Draft the review in the vault's language and local format. Preview substantial writes and structural actions, then wait for approval unless the structure contract permits the update as mechanical.

Never schedule itself or create an automation. Finish when the chosen object or period has an evidence-backed assessment, unresolved gaps are explicit, and the next review condition follows the local method contract.
