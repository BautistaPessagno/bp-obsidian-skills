# Practice or evaluation review

Use the course or local authority defined by the method contract. Gather attempted problems, explanations produced without notes, mistakes, feedback, rubric results, and transfer cases that match the evaluation's scope and notation.

Judge readiness by what the user can retrieve, solve, explain, and transfer under relevant conditions. Distinguish a missing attempt from a failed attempt. Identify the smallest gap that limits readiness and propose the next diagnostic problem or practice, not a broad activity target.
