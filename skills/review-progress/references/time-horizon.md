# Time-horizon review

Use the exact horizon and prompts defined by the local method contract. If it supports the common Daily, Weekly, and Monthly names, keep their questions distinct:

- **Daily** asks what was attempted, what evidence appeared, what remains unclear, and what needs attention next.
- **Weekly** compares several attempts, looks for repeated gaps or transfer across contexts, and chooses the next meaningful test or application.
- **Monthly** examines durable change, applications that held up, recurring friction, and whether the review method itself needs adjustment.

Do not infer learning from the number of notes, completed tasks, or hours. Use those figures only to explain constraints or patterns around retrieval, transfer, and application evidence.
