# Local contract shape

Draft these contracts from evidence in the active vault. The headings may follow local style, but every decision below must be findable by another agent.

## Structure contract

Record:

- the vault root and the hard boundary for searches, edits, and links;
- the selected agent guide and the location of these two contracts;
- the default response language and any path-specific language rule;
- actual note locations and how an agent recognizes each kind of object;
- templates and when they apply;
- existing properties, their allowed values, and whether they map to the conceptual states "developed" or "learned";
- indexes, `.base` files, and other navigation mechanisms;
- link, embed, heading, alias, and filename conventions;
- mechanical edits the agent may apply directly;
- semantic rewrites, creation, splitting, moving, and renaming rules that require a preview and approval;
- unresolved or conflicting conventions.

## Method contract

Map each common concept to local terms and real objects:

- **capture**: unprocessed material;
- **intent**: thought, decision, reference, learning, or a local equivalent;
- **source**: external or local authority used to support a claim;
- **durable knowledge**: material developed enough to retrieve and use;
- **practice**: an attempt that produces evidence;
- **application**: an observable use outside passive review;
- **evidence**: what counts as proof of retrieval, transfer, application, or a gap;
- **review**: the vault's supported objects, horizons, prompts, and destinations for outcomes.

Also record the local source hierarchy, how to show conflicts, how to distinguish fact, interpretation, and experience, and where sparse learning evidence belongs. Define the local meanings of "developed" and "learned" separately. "Learned" requires observed retrieval and transfer; a polished note alone does not qualify.

For each review mode, specify its trigger, evidence sources, questions, output location, and next-review convention. Include Daily, Weekly, Monthly, practice, or evaluation only when they exist in this vault.
